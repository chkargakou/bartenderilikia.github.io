# Cosmopolitan (Chris' Edit)
![[./images/20261004212002.png]]
### Ingredients
- 45 ml Vodka
- 30 ml Cointreau
- 15 ml Lime Juice
- 30 ml Bio Cranberry Juice
- 10 ml Simple Syrup
- Tiniest pinch of salt
### Instructions
- Pour everything inside the shaker with ice.
- Shake vigorously for 10-15 seconds.
- Strain into chilled martini or coup glass.
- Garnish with a lemon twist or lime wheel.
- Enjoy.
